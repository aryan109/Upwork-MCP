Follow Claude v2, with stricter execution

Synthesis of six independent reviews of the Gemini strategy, Claude v1, Claude v2, and the Upwork MCP specification. All six were written on 4 October 2026.

The path. Sell Claude setup for business teams as fixed-price packages. Show $65 per hour on the profile. Start every new client with a Setup Sprint or an Audit. Use the Upwork MCP server only to find jobs and draft proposals that you confirm yourself. Treat Gemini’s enterprise architect position as the destination you earn into after new contracts support it. Keep that headline, the $120–$150 rate, and the $3,000 floor off the profile this week.

This week, in order

Day 1 — profile, spend nothing

Title: Claude AI Implementation | Claude Code, MCP & Agents. Confirm it fits the title field; Upwork’s limit is believed to be near 70 characters. First two lines name the buyer and the outcome, plus Top Rated and 100% Job Success.

Set the rate to $65. Turn the availability badge on. Decline the 31 August instructor invite unless the pay and scope are specific. Reconnect the Upwork MCP server under the Aryan P. login. Leave profile boost off. Buy Connects on the day applications start.

Days 2–3 — proof, then warm clients

Record one two-minute demo of Claude running a real workflow. Portfolio: that demo, the strongest real agent or automation project, and this MCP repository labeled as a technical sample.

Message the repeat enterprise client first, in the existing room, with the Setup Sprint. Then other clients who left a clear 5.0 and for whom the sprint fits. Replace the OpenClaw listings with the sprint. Submit the audit after the sprint is in review.

Days 4–7 — only jobs that pass

Search in the 18:30–23:30 IST window. Many days will have one to three real targets. A good week is about 15 to 20 tailored proposals if the pool supports it.

Hard skips: unverified payment, off-platform pay, unpaid tests, commission-only, impossible scope, a location that excludes you, and a single seat that is already hired. Strong skip: average hourly paid under $25, and clients who leave complaint-heavy 4.0–4.5 reviews.

After 20 proposals

If fewer than about 6 were opened, change the first two lines and who you target. If they are opened and nobody replies, lead with the audit or a smaller first milestone.

Compare Claude, broader AI automation, and n8n as search clusters. Raise the displayed rate to $80 only after three completed contracts at $65 are visible. Keep the rate fixed inside a running contract.

Keep from Gemini, and leave out

Keep

Funded escrow before any build. Written acceptance criteria and two revision rounds. Risks and limits stated in the proposal — one 3.8 review said the score would have been higher if risks had been clearer. A recorded handover. Milestones split so architecture is approved before implementation. Architecture language (private MCP, permissions, human approval) belongs inside the later implementation offer.

Leave out

Enterprise AI Architect as the headline. A $3,000 cold floor. Raw MCP boilerplates on Project Catalog. Zero-leakage, HIPAA, or GxP guarantees. The “8 years” claim beside a 2017–2021 degree. Asking clients for a five-star rating. Unattended bidding. Names scraped from other people’s reviews. Using this repository as a client case study.

Confirm on the live account before you change it. Consultation status, the exact title character limit, Job Success wording for the 90-day repeat rule, badge cost, Connects price, and whether the Top Rated removal perk is scarce. The MCP specification claims 51 tools and lists 48, and its smart_search parameters may not match the live schema. Read tool help after you reconnect as Aryan P.
