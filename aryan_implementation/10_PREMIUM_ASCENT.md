# 10 — Premium Ascent (Phase 6: $120+/hr, $8K–$25K engagements)

The Gemini "Enterprise AI Architect" plan is the **destination**, not the starting point. This file defines what must be true before Aryan repositions upward, what the premium storefront looks like, and how Upwork feeds Revedor.

## 2. Premium storefront (apply only after G6)

- **Title (≤70):** `AI Systems Architect | Claude, Agents, MCP & Automation for Operations Teams` (72 — trim "& Automation" if needed `[VERIFY LIVE]`) or `Enterprise AI Architect | Claude Agents, MCP, RAG & Workflow Systems`.
- **Rate:** $120 → $150/hr; consultations $150 / 45 min (Gemini figure).
- **Offers:** AI Operating System build $8K–$25K (4 milestones: discovery & architecture 20% / core build 40% / integrations & agents 25% / rollout, training, hypercare 15%); Fractional AI Lead retainer $3K–$6K/month; Architecture Review $2,500 fixed (replaces the $750/$1,500 Audit).
- **Milestone safeguards (Gemini):** no build before a funded architecture milestone; written acceptance criteria per milestone; change-request pricing published.
- **Catalog:** retire Setup Sprint Starter ($299); keep Team tier ($1,200) as the smallest entry; Audit becomes Architecture Review.
- **Overview:** keep the Problem→Outcome→Proof→Offer structure; proof becomes three case studies with numbers and the Care Plan clients' quotes; language stays plain, still no "guaranteed", still no compliance absolutes unless a certification exists `[VERIFY LIVE]`.

## 3. Positioning shift
From "I help businesses turn Claude, AI agents and automation into reliable business workflows" to "I design and run the AI operating layer for operations-heavy companies: Claude agents, MCP integrations and the governance around them." Same promise, bigger unit of work, buyer is now a COO/Head of Ops rather than a founder/manager.

## 4. Upwork → Revedor handoff
- Every L4/L5 client is a Revedor case-study candidate (with permission) and a referral source.
- Jobs above $25K or needing a team → quote through Revedor (off-platform only if the client relationship started off-platform; Upwork-originated clients stay on Upwork for 24 months per ToS `[VERIFY LIVE: current non-circumvention terms and the conversion fee option]`).
- Weekly: tag `revedor_candidate=true` on jobs/clients in `jobs.json` when the post reveals multi-team scope; monthly: review the list for Revedor outreach themes (what buyers ask for, what they pay).

## 5. What not to do before G6
- Do not raise the sticker to $120 on the strength of the old $120 setting; the market showed only 9 of 313 Claude-title hourly jobs allowed ≥ $100/hr (2026-10-04 snapshot).
- Do not drop the Sprint/Audit rungs; they are the lead-in even for premium buyers.
- Do not describe yourself as "enterprise" without an enterprise contract you can cite.
- Do not spend the Top Rated feedback-removal perk on old feedback.
